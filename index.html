CHAPTER 02 — THE TECHNOLOGY

Solar Atmospheric
Hydro-Extraction.

AQUASOURCE technology harvests moisture directly from atmospheric humidity—even in arid regions with as low as 15% humidity—using solar-powered condensation and graphene nano-filtration.

☀

STEP 01

Solar Capture

Aerospace-grade photovoltaic envelope powers the extraction cycle autonomously.

💧

STEP 02

Dew Point Condensation

Peltier thermoelectric core reaches dew point, pulling water molecules from air.

⬡

STEP 03

Graphene Nano-Filtration

Multi-stage graphene membrane removes 99.99% contaminants at molecular level.

✦

STEP 04

Mineral Remineralization

Essential minerals are reintroduced, producing crisp, clean, nourishing drinking water.
